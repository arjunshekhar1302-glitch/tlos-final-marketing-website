Metric 1: Financial Return (50 pts)

Final Net Worth = Cash + ∑(share qty × frozen final price).

50 × (N - Rank) / (N - 1)
Rank 1 = 50 pts, Last = 0 pts. Ties get average rank.
Metric 2: Decision Quality (30 pts)

6 events × 5 pts each. Evaluated using team's holdings before that event.

5 × (Chosen - Worst) / (Best - Worst)
2.5 pts if 0 affected shares owned or Best == Worst.
Metric 3: Risk Management (20 pts)

10 Diversification + 10 Downside Control.

Div: 3 cos=4, 4 cos=6, 5 cos=8, 6 cos=10 pts.
Downside: 10 × (1 - TeamLoss / MaxLoss) across 3 Bad Events.
Judge Bonus (0–3 pts, Top 5 Only)

Awarded only to Top 5 teams by Automatic Score.

Clarity (0/1) + Justification (0/1) + Q&A (0/1).
Multi-judge avg rounded to nearest whole integer.
